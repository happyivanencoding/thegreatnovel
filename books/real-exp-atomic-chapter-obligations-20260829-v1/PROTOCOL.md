# Atomic Chapter Obligations｜Experiment Protocol v0.2

## Goal

Test whether a deterministic Atomic Gate can make Paragraph-Delta Reviser safe enough to reduce Reviser wall-clock without sacrificing top male-oriented commercial quality or long-form authority integrity.

## Frozen route

Control:

```text
Frozen Mission / Authority
→ Terra-high Primary
→ Luna-high Full Authority Reviser
```

Treatment:

```text
Frozen Mission / Authority
→ deterministic Atomic Pack
→ Terra-high Primary
→ Luna-high Paragraph Delta
→ local patch apply
→ deterministic Atomic Gate
    ├─ pass: adopt candidate
    └─ fail: Full Luna-high Reviser
```

No LLM classifier may decide adoption.

## Calibration layers

1. **Boundary unit tests**: safe and unsafe synthetic clauses, including negation, pending vs terminal, original/copy, actor/object, power and commercial preservation.
2. **Known-bad mutation recall**: historical prose mutations must fail.
3. **Historical Full shadow**: current control is audited but is not labeled safe gold.
4. **20-chapter shadow coverage**: report preflight eligible, fail closed and residual failures.
5. **Real model route**: run Delta, local apply, Gate, fallback-adjusted timing.
6. **Independent repeat**: exact route status, final text and operation-set consensus.
7. **Final-draft blind**: commercial Reader and Authority / Canon separately.
8. **Cross-book static**: verify parser/schema generalization before production claims.
9. **Explicit residual repair**: if both Delta and historical Full fail Gate, give Full Reviser the concrete failing obligations and count the entire extra call.

## Primary boundaries

- current Primary / Frozen Mission actor identity outranks remote Book/Human names;
- negated, planned, conditional and deadline actions are not occurred events;
- original, copy, title, possession, usage right and custody are distinct;
- received, entitlement, pending, lost and disputed are distinct resource states;
- battle scale does not imply stable power transition;
- unresolved facts prohibit revelation but do not require repeated “unknown” wording;
- Human cue is named and trigger-specific;
- commercial value is preserve-if-present, not a mandatory addition quota;
- unsupported language and source conflict fail closed.

## Production gate

No default route change unless:

- known-bad recall has no material blind spot;
- 20-chapter and cross-book coverage is sufficient without synonym guessing;
- final-draft Reader and Authority are both non-inferior;
- independent repeat shows stable route/adoption behavior;
- fallback-adjusted critical path remains materially faster;
- residual Full repair closes all obligations;
- no new permanent LLM classifier or reviewer is added.
