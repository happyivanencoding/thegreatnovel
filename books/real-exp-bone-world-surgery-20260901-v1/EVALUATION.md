# Bone World 11—20 Surgery — Final Evaluation

## Verdict

**DIRECTIONAL PASS** for the Story Engine correction; **not approved as a direct production-book replacement**.

The experiment proves that the Bone World can stop using logistics / registration / route administration as foreground while preserving its best fantasy and character causality. It also proves that the freed narrative space can carry main-world consequences. However, the realized Treatment compresses too aggressively: the clean version falls from **23,328 to 12,154 non-whitespace characters (-47.9%)**, well beyond the intended ~25–30% reduction / roughly seven-to-eight-chapter density. The chapters still read as scenes rather than a pure synopsis, but several are short enough that this version should remain an experiment rather than replace the existing 40-ch book.

## Density

- Live final Chapters 11—20: **23,328 non-whitespace characters**.
- Clean Treatment: **12,154 non-whitespace characters**.
- Reduction: **47.9%**.
- Structural allocation: Chapters **11—18 Bone World**, Chapters **19—20 main-world consequences**.

UTF-8 byte counts are not used as the primary comparison because newline normalization changes byte totals without changing story density; non-whitespace characters are stable across that formatting difference.

## What the Treatment successfully removes from foreground

- the long foreigner-registration / joint-inspection sequence becomes one decision-bearing encounter;
- business-registration, guarantee, bone-ticket and inheritance paperwork is reduced to the result that changes Shang Wu's choices;
- repeated cart / wheel / rope / route implementation is collapsed into one or two concrete failures instead of becoming the chapter engine;
- post-climax route ownership, fees and city / merchant governance are reduced to outcome-level facts rather than another administrative arc;
- repeated explanation of already-established power boundaries is much lower than baseline.

## What remains strong

- **Bone World lived fantasy**: clothing, armor, beds, carts and bodies leave room for what an old wound may regrow into; the newborn hospital makes the rule part of ordinary life rather than only combat tech.
- **Crack-bone wind** remains a physical pressure on both people and transport rather than an exposition concept.
- **He Chengu truly wins** the North Rib Fork and four carts of reverse-moon salt; the protagonist's ability does not erase the loss.
- **Shang Wu remains a Living Actor**: she cuts her old family route, tells Ning Jin to save the salt before changing her mind when both bodies are collapsing, later chooses the independent route for herself, and does not become his follower.
- **Spirit-Sea 4** is still a normal Xuanyao breakthrough financed by existing resources / combat / recovery, not a free Bone-World tier-up.
- **Wind-marrow double openings** still come from the same old wound really regrowing under two different environments. The climax is bodily: crack-bone wind on one real body, hot marrow pressure on the other, irreversible new bone openings, solid matter still cannot cross, and both real bodies remain genuine points of failure.

## Return consequence

Chapters 19—20 no longer behave as `return → confirm power → next door`.

Two strong old lines produce new facts:

1. **Tianwai / main-world price**: after ordinary travel from the canonical red-brown-mountain return point to Nine-Ring Tiandu, Shang Wang treats Ning Jin as a different kind of long-term bargaining counterparty after seeing a new public consequence. He does not learn the hidden permanence / trigger.
2. **Pei Zhaolin / White-Day Heart**: he remains Spirit-Sea 9 versus Ning Jin's Spirit-Sea 4, but changes from his old surrounding tactic to separating the two real positions and forcing simultaneous consumption. This is a genuine Rival update, not `仍然存在`.

The old-deed material is deliberately weaker: it is used as a **life-root cue**, not falsely upgraded into a family mystery. Ning Jin only revisits the already-established fact that the old deed was once his last valuable possession and chooses to keep it now that his economic position is radically different. The clean Treatment removes the raw model's invented buyer / delivery / house-condition details.

## Authority drift caught and corrected

The raw Sol plan silently moved the return point to Nine-Ring Tiandu and assumed an accessible repairable old house. The prose prompt corrected the first before generation, and the clean Treatment removes the remaining invented old-deed buyer / delivery details. The final experiment therefore keeps:

- black-gate return first to the **red-brown Xuanyao mountains**;
- normal travel to Tiandu;
- old-deed origin / property condition / family history unknown;
- no new named future treasure-meeting qualification;
- no future-world rule / person / reward prewrite.

## Residual weakness

The experiment overshoots compression. Chapters 19—20 now carry the right kind of story, but the ten-chapter treatment as a whole is closer to **half** the baseline textual density than the desired 70–75%. The correct production lesson is therefore **Supporting Logic Compression + Story-Bearing Reallocation**, not `make chapters shorter`.

A future content rewrite of the book should restore story-bearing texture, relationship beats, public proof pressure and action-space consequences until the Bone World lands closer to the intended 25–30% reduction. No new architecture, reviewer, density gate or per-chapter word quota is justified by this experiment.
