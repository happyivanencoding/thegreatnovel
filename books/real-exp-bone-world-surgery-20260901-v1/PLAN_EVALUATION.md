# Bone World Surgery Plan — Evaluation

## Verdict

**DIRECTIONAL PASS** before prose realization.

The Sol-high plan correctly compresses the Bone World into eight story-bearing chapters and spends Chapters 19–20 on main-world consequences. It protects the world/body fantasy, the North Rib Fork loss, the normal Spirit-Sea 4 breakthrough, the newborn hospital, the return-injury choice, and the wind-marrow double-opening climax while removing most logistics/governance foreground.

## Strong structural changes

- Chapter 11: the three-times price / object inspection is compressed into one decision-bearing encounter instead of a registration sequence.
- Chapters 12–15: crack-bone wind, He Chengu's closed route, the shared-spirit-sea limit, and `商妩 vs 四车盐` remain foreground.
- Chapter 16: the newborn hospital carries the lived-world consequence through bodies, clothing, beds and treatment rather than an institutional taxonomy lecture.
- Chapters 17–18: Ning Jin refuses the safe salt seal, puts the same old injury into two real environments, and obtains `风髓双口` through irreversible bodily risk. Route ownership is collapsed to outcome-level aftermath.
- Chapters 19–20: space formerly spent on salt-road administration is reassigned to Tianwai repricing, Pei Zhaolin changing tactics, and a low-frequency old-deed choice.

## Authority corrections required before prose

The raw plan contains two details that are not safe to adopt literally:

1. It silently moves the Bone World return point from the live baseline's **red-brown Xuanyao mountains** to the outskirts of Nine-Ring Tiandu. The prose treatment must restore the red-brown-mountain return and use ordinary travel/time passage before any Tiandu contact.
2. It expands the known `旧宅契` into an immediately accessible, repairable old house. Existing Canon only establishes Ning Jin as a Ning-family branch member and the deed as the last valuable thing he once carried. The prose treatment may change how he treats the deed, but must not invent the house's condition, origin, dispossession history, or a family tragedy.

The plan also proposes a named future `天洲级宝物会`. This is not needed to prove Behavioral Repricing. The prose treatment should prefer an immediate change in price / bargaining relationship / treatment over inventing a new qualification or named future event.

These corrections are encoded in `run_prose.py`; the raw Sol plan remains untouched as experiment evidence.

## Target density

Live final Chapters 11–20 contain **23,328 non-whitespace characters / 74,618 UTF-8 bytes**. The content experiment targets about **16.1k–17.5k non-whitespace characters**, approximately a 25–30% reduction, while allowing Chapter 18 to remain one of the longer chapters because it carries the actual body-fantasy climax.
