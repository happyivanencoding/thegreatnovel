## Root Problem

The old opening had enough backstage facts, but reader knowledge was weaker than system knowledge.

The core failure was not simply “too little exposition”. It was three separate compression losses:

- **Texture Present / Rule Missing**: firepits, shadows and terms existed, but the reader was still asked to infer public rules.
- **World Ruler Without Social Meaning**: tier effects could appear without explaining what the next tier means in ordinary life/status.
- **Named Opportunity Compression**: Story Program already defined `铜羽商盟公开试场 → 随队护送契约 / 预付款 / 离乡机会`, but a Future 10 item could compress it to `试场前训练 / 公开机会`, after which Director/Writer no longer knew why the opportunity mattered.

## Stable Production Fix

### A. Public World Knowledge

World Vision now explicitly separates:

- public/common knowledge that a normal resident already knows and the reader may be told directly;
- hidden causes, backstage relations and future reveals that remain unknown.

For an obviously unfamiliar/fantasy world, Chapter 1 may receive a small Public Common-Knowledge Reader Release bundle. The bundle is not a per-chapter quota and normally covers only 2–3 different functions that matter to the first few chapters, such as:

- main power + current/next ruler;
- daily danger / shared life rule;
- approved public social position / value benchmark.

Primary may use 1–3 short direct exposition paragraphs. Props, atmosphere and imagery can make the world vivid but do not count as a substitute for the rule itself.

Authority Reviser now treats `texture present / rule missing` as an incomplete scheduled public Reader Release and may restore the minimum direct clarification.

### B. Named Opportunity Value

Named opportunity value stays on the Story/Plan authority chain, not the World Reader Release chain.

The important production recovery is conditional and deterministic:

`Story Program / Outline Long Block → Future 10 Chapter Plan → Director`

If the current chapter plan already mentions an opportunity family (`试场 / 选拔 / 招募 / 契约 ...`) but has compressed away the value, runtime searches only the **current Long Block** for one matching already-approved line that contains both the opportunity and a concrete value/result. It injects that line to Director as:

`当前具名机会权威（从当前剧情块确定性恢复；不是新事实）`

It does **not**:

- create a reward;
- predict that the protagonist will win;
- read a future chapter for a new fact;
- add a new model call;
- create a ledger or new schema.

### C. Re-contact Means Re-contact

Frozen Power says the two bodies do not share experience while separate and experience returns only after re-contact / merge.

The first otherwise-good final draft invented a remote recall: the distant shadow dissolved and travelled back across town. That is an unauthorized mechanism.

The final Authority Reviser contract therefore also states that an authority such as `重新接触 / 合并` cannot be expanded into remote recall, cross-distance shadow recovery, or merge without meeting.

The final R2 draft fixes this by making the second body physically return to the martial hall; the two bodies touch palms, their shadows overlap, and only then do fatigue and experience return.

## Reader Restatement Test on Final R3

A first-time reader can answer directly:

1. **What power does this world use?** 影力，修炼人的影子。
2. **What can first tier do?** Stretch/harden shadow into short blades, ropes, or a defensive block.
3. **What does second tier mean?** Shadow-body protection and short-distance pulling; an ordinary second-tier is already good enough for city guard, hunting-team backbone or caravan guard work.
4. **Why are fixed firepits / stable shadows common?** They are ordinary life infrastructure and help prevent shadow beasts from approaching a person through bad shadow conditions.
5. **Where is Gu Linchuan at the opening?** He has passed the shadow-affinity test but has not yet completed first-tier condensation.
6. **Where is he after the chapter result?** The prose directly names the state change: he has completed `一阶凝影`.
7. **Why does the Copper Feather public trial matter?** A good result can bring a caravan escort contract, an advance payment and a real chance to leave the town.
8. **What is still unknown?** Hidden causes and future World reveals remain unrevealed; clarity did not become an encyclopedia.
