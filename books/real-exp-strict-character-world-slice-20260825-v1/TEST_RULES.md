# Test Rules

- Reuse the exact same protagonist-blind 澜生界 from the previous architecture experiment; no new World LLM call.
- Never expose active named events, named NPCs, named locations, named mysteries, Reader Coordinates, or future story routes to the strict Character generator.
- Preserve failed/over-corrected variants instead of silently replacing them.
- v1 tests Story Opportunity isolation; v3 additionally splits Core Fantasy authority (`POWER_BASELINE`) from upbringing authority (`LIFE_CONTEXT`); v4 restores the product invariant that this is a male-oriented progression novel whose protagonist must genuinely become stronger over time.
- Power exception must be generated from World Power Normal/Rarity, not from profession.
- Upbringing may shape discovery, attitude and use of the exception, but may not determine the exception by occupational analogy.
- Growth Compatibility must preserve both ordinary cultivation progression and anomaly mastery, plus a permanent high-level boundary.
- NO Story Program, Outline, chapters, or LLM judge until human audit approves the Character layer.
- Audit questions: relative specialness, formative causality, hook leakage, character diversity, commercial fantasy appetite, profession→ability collapse, personality memorability, and long-form growth compatibility.
