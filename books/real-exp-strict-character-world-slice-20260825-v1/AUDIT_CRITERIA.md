# Audit Criteria

Evaluate A (previous opportunity-leaking context) vs B (strict world slice) on:

1. Relative specialness: does the ability/identity clearly deviate from an explicit World Normal?
2. Formative causality: do concrete world-conditioned experiences visibly cause adaptations and later behavior?
3. Hook leakage: does the character appear custom-built for a named world event, mystery, NPC, place, or artifact?
4. Character diversity: do the candidates feel like different life trajectories rather than one social-growth hero in different skins?
5. Commercial fantasy appetite: is there a simple, desirable, scene-bearing fantasy rather than only a clever system?
6. Engineering / governance collapse: does the character become a technician, optimizer, contract solver, or reformer by default?
7. Character IP potential: is there a memorable person even before Story Program supplies plot?
