## Root cause

The five-line watch asks Terra to self-police facts that are not equally available in its context. Primary has Mission, Canon prose and Curator projection, but the Reviser additionally receives full Frozen Power Core, full Frozen Human Core, safe World Authority, Reader Release checklist and Canon Index in a high-salience remote-Authority layout. A generic self-check cannot substitute for missing or low-salience facts.

Therefore do not add more checklist lines. The next hypothesis is deterministic **Final Facts Projection**, not a sixth self-review rule.
