# Prose Delta Composition Final A/B

Date: 2026-09-05

## Verdict

**PASS / production freeze candidate accepted.**

The final clean experiment held the exact same Authority-closed control prose constant and changed only a deterministic composition of previously generated exact-local prose patches.

Reader blind result after direct classic-original reading: **Treatment 3/3 wins**.

Authority blind result after reading actual Frozen Authority: **3/3 TIE; 0 Treatment-only hard findings**.

## Final independent variable

Control:

`same immutable Primary → same Sol-high Authority Delta → final prose`

Treatment:

`same immutable Primary → same Sol-high Authority Delta → deterministically apply only exact-local Prose patches whose OLD still occurs exactly once after Authority repair`

No Primary regeneration and no second Sol sample occurred in the final A/B.

## Reader blind results

- fast: Treatment=A, Judge chose A.
- shadow: Treatment=A, Judge chose A.
- pace: Treatment=B, Judge chose B.

All three Reader Judges directly read local classic originals; GBrain used as original evidence: NO.

Representative mechanisms identified by the judges:

- remove immediate abstract restatement after action/reaction already proves the meaning;
- merge mechanically fragmented paragraphs belonging to one continuous beat;
- preserve concrete objects, social response, relationship friction, and scene thickness while reducing author-side interpretation.

## Failed intermediate routes and what they proved

### Whole fresh Primary prompt pressure

Adding the prose rule directly to Primary caused whole-draft resampling. Reader quality sometimes improved, but event identity, amounts, locations, or backstory could drift independently. This route is not clean enough to attribute or freeze.

### Prose Delta followed by a newly sampled Sol Authority Delta

The Prose Delta itself was clean, but re-running Sol after it produced a different Authority-repair sample. That reintroduced Authority variance unrelated to the prose change.

### Final composition rule

The successful route separates the two concerns:

`immutable Primary → [Terra Prose Delta || Sol Authority Delta] → Authority-first deterministic composition`

Both Deltas read the same immutable Primary. Authority is applied first. A Prose patch is applied only if its exact OLD still occurs once in the Authority-closed text; otherwise it is skipped. Prose therefore cannot overwrite an Authority repair and does not trigger a second Sol sample.

## Production freeze

Freeze:

- Batch Prose Delta: GPT-5.6 Terra high, raw GBrain OFF.
- Scope: only immediate abstract restatement, same-beat paragraph fragmentation, and functional short Q/A continuity.
- Preservation boundary: no new/deleted Story beat, history, number, reward, power, item, knowledge, motive, or relationship state.
- Batch Authority Delta remains GPT-5.6 Sol high, raw GBrain OFF.
- Both Deltas read the same immutable Primary.
- Deterministic merge is Authority first; Prose conflicts are skipped rather than repaired by another LLM call.
- State runs only after composed final prose is fixed.

Do not freeze per-chapter prose quotas, parent/family requirements, generic dialogue-length rules, or a whole-book shortening KPI.

## Evaluation-method correction

Reader/classic Judges must not infer Authority from anonymous A/B differences when they have not read Frozen Authority. Literary Reader judging and Authority auditing are separate evidence lanes. Authority verdicts require the actual Frozen World / Character / Story / BOOK / Outline / Canon for the sampled window.
