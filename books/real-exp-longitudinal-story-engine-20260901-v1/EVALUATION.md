# Longitudinal Story Engine — Real Treatment Evaluation

## Scope

This experiment holds the existing 《我身藏诸界》 book state / Current Character / approved World Expansion inputs and reruns Story Refresh with the new production longitudinal directions. It evaluates structure, not whether a stochastic rerun reproduces every previously preferred local acquisition.

## 21—30 Treatment

**Verdict: DIRECTIONAL PASS.**

The baseline keeps Pei Zhaolin, Shang Wu, Jing Li / Lan Sheng and other long lines in carry-forward state, but largely phrases them as `仍存在 / 继续压力 / 本轮不强制回访`. The Treatment creates new observable facts instead:

- Pei Zhaolin remains Spirit-Sea 9 vs Ning Jin Spirit-Sea 4, but changes how he attacks after observing a new public consequence; the Rival line therefore changes the protagonist's future decision surface rather than merely remaining named.
- Bone-World salt-route consequences enter later causality instead of existing only as a static 30% asset.
- Main-world/Tianwai social pricing changes after the return rather than resetting directly into the next world.

It does **not** become a replacement Story Program because the stochastic rerun changes the local core acquisition from the production-proven `未尽续行` to another acquisition. The structural result supports the new longitudinal rule; the local content is not adopted.

## 31—40 Treatment

**Verdict: DIRECTIONAL PASS.**

The baseline has a visible Task Board shape: Lu Yizhu / Su Wuyan / Ruan Qiniang each expose a route and reward, then Ning Jin compares them. The Treatment materially changes that structure:

- Ruan Qiniang is already meeting her lover, Ye Huisha is already moving the three-shadow child away from the Gui-Ri army, and Lu Yizhu is already dismantling/moving the hanging-birth院 before Ning Jin chooses anything.
- These actions collide on the same shadow route / place / time window, and one action interrupts another.
- Ning Jin's route emerges from being caught in the collision and from his own body-risk / curiosity, not from three NPCs waiting for a menu choice.

This is not merely Task Board vocabulary replacement. However, the stochastic rerun replaces the production-proven `双影为邻` with another local acquisition, so the Treatment is structural evidence only and is not adopted as the new 31—40 content.

## Persistent Global Progress Ruler

**Verdict: PASS.**

Neither real Story Refresh Treatment produced an unauthorized staged skill tree. The dedicated 100-ch test further constrained the coordinate to:

- `Current Meta Capability`: what black-gate / cross-world behavior has actually happened and what permanent Advantage Stack already exists;
- `Next Observable Question`: the next externally checkable question, not a promised future competence.

The result explicitly did **not** pre-authorize carrying people, fixed teleportation, world merging, or other future stages.

## 100-ch Book-Level Spine Test

**Verdict: PASS.**

Without generating any future world, future chapter number, future reward, future ability or Mystery truth, Sol-high extracted four viable cross-Horizon spines from Chapter-40 Canon:

1. Xuanyao public ruler / Pei Zhaolin / Tianwai repricing versus Ning Jin's growing cross-world Advantage Stack;
2. black-gate Meta Mystery and new evidence without deciding its truth;
3. old-world relationships/assets becoming active consequences rather than static inventory;
4. the Ning-family branch / old deed / `凭什么只拿一半` life-root line, while explicitly keeping family history unknown.

The first two are strong enough to carry 60—120 chapters; the latter two provide relationship/economic compounding and a protagonist life pull outside the ability itself. Future World Expansion remains protagonist-blind and only the later Re-Collision chooses which spine naturally intersects the new horizon.
