# Split Power Seed × Human Seed Experiment — Results

## Verdict

**Split authority PASS. Human appetite diversity PARTIAL PASS. Do not spend Sol yet.**

The experiment reuses the exact same protagonist-blind `澜生界` and the exact same four v4 Power cores. Only one new GPT-5.6 Luna high Human batch was generated.

## What changed

v4 used one Character inference:

`Power Normal → Exception → Core Fantasy → Upbringing → Adaptation → Behavior`

This allowed post-hoc semantic rhyme inside Character: an unfinished-spell ability acquired a childhood full of unfinished things; a two-anchor spatial ability acquired a two-home childhood.

v5 freezes two independent authorities:

- **Power Seed**: anomaly, reader fantasy, growth grammar. In this A/B it is extracted deterministically from v4 and frozen before Human generation.
- **Human Seed**: upbringing, formative causality, current desire, Core Obsession, Excess, Behavior Signature, relationships, Character Hook. It sees only `LIFE_CONTEXT + Human GBrain`; it sees no Power content and no Story Opportunities.

The final Character Card is deterministic concatenation. No Character Composer is allowed to reconcile the two seeds.

## Hard isolation result

Human generation leakage check:

- frozen Power terms / names: **0 hits**
- named Story Opportunity terms: **0 hits**

Pairing is fixed by index. No model selects a compatible Human for a Power.

## Human Seeds

1. **季衡／把名字钉在纸上** — contract-scribe household; wants durable proof/ownership; will complicate his own life to establish what is true and traceable.
2. **冯渡／先拥有一条船** — harbor/ship household; wants ownership and expanding movement assets; willingly delays comfort to own ships, berths and routes.
3. **魏执／我要让别人按我的判断活下来** — ordinary military household; wants genuine decision authority and responsibility; seeks situations where success/failure can be visibly attributed to his judgement.
4. **林问川／不接受“大概”** — medicine-shop household; wants to understand why bodies fail and make his judgement indispensable; will sacrifice profitable opportunities to resolve unexplained cases.

## v4 → v5 post-hoc-rhyme comparison

### Pair 1
Power: `借万物一锋 / 复合借相`  
Human: `季衡 / evidence, ownership, traceability`

There is a weak shared preference for concrete support/evidence, but the Human biography no longer explains why the supernatural anomaly exists. **Improved.**

### Pair 2
Power: `留着未完 / 未完承载`  
Human: `冯渡 / own a ship, berth, routes, keep moving`

The Human seed contains no unfinished-life metaphor. Power and Human are clearly separate creative sources. **Strong pass.**

### Pair 3
Power: `受力成路 / 受力留向`  
Human: `魏执 / decision authority, responsibility, command`

The two sources are independent but can later create useful tension: a Human obsessed with making others trust his judgement is paired with a Power whose strength depends on personally encountering force. This chemistry was not pre-explained. **Pass.**

### Pair 4
Power: `两处同身 / 双锚驻身`  
Human: `林问川 / diagnostic certainty and unexplained illness`

This is the clearest proof of architectural separation. No two-home biography, no forced-choice life philosophy, no spatial metaphor in childhood. **Strong pass.**

## Psychological-distribution result

The old v4 batch strongly converged on autonomy / anti-control:

- do not make me an asset;
- do not finish my choices for me;
- do not treat me as expendable;
- do not force me to choose one place.

The v5 Human batch is more diverse:

- proof / ownership;
- acquisitive mobility and asset expansion;
- command / decision authority;
- epistemic certainty / diagnosis.

So the explicit `Core Obsession + Excess + batch-collapse` changes helped.

However, the distribution is **not fully open yet**. All four still lean toward rational, legible, manageable desires: evidence, assets, decision rights, correct diagnosis. There is relatively little wasteful appetite, vanity, sensory pleasure, rivalry-for-its-own-sake, romantic fixation, aesthetic obsession, or other non-instrumental life force.

This residual should **not** be fixed by reconnecting Power and Human. It belongs only to future Human Seed craft, likely by restoring the useful core of old `Narrative Appetite Before Defensive Balance` without giving the model a personality menu.

## Schema changes now justified

### Power Seed
Keep / support:
- World Power Normal → Legal Exception
- Core Fantasy
- Growth Compatibility
- High-Tier Mutation
- Legendary Trajectory
- non-Canon Future Legend Image

For this A/B, the last three new ambition fields were intentionally **not backfilled** into frozen v4 Power, to avoid changing the Power variable.

### Human Seed
Keep:
- initial world/social position
- Formative Facts → Adaptation → Observable Behavior
- current private desire
- Core Obsession
- Excess
- Behavior Signature
- important relationship origins
- Character Hook

Do **not** put Power, first Power reveal, world reaction to Power, or `world cannot digest him` into Human Canon.

### Character Card
For now: deterministic `POWER CORE + HUMAN CORE` only. Do not add an LLM Composer.

### Collision Authority
Only here should the system first decide:
- how this Human discovers/uses this Power;
- how pre-existing relationships react;
- which Story Opportunities collide with them;
- what the first actual reveal scene is;
- how the world changes after real events.

## Cost / next step

This experiment used no new World, no new Power, no Sol, no Outline and no chapter generation. It used one fresh Luna high Human batch.

The architecture is now strong enough to justify a **single** future Sol Collision test, but because Human appetite still shows a rationality bias, the recommended order is to let the user audit these four Human Seeds / pairings first before spending Sol.
